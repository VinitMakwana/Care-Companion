Our promise

We are careful so
you can feel safe.

Care Companion is a caring information companion, not a replacement for doctors. It is built with the principle that being safe is more important than being fast.

🤍
No scary diagnosis
We never say you definitely have something. We share general information and encourage you to see a doctor for personal advice.
🚨
Emergencies heard immediately
If you mention serious symptoms, we immediately guide you toward appropriate emergency help.
💊
Medicines handled with care
We don't create new prescriptions. We help you understand the ones your doctor has already given you.
