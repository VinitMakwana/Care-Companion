Important Safety Information
Care Companion is an AI health information assistant, not a medical professional. All content is for educational purpose only and does NOT replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. No definitive diagnosis, no new prescriptions. Always consult qualified healthcare provider.

🌿 What you can expect

Care Companion is designed to make health information easier to understand and everyday care easier to manage.
  • Clear answers: Get simple explanations about symptoms, medicines, and everyday health questions.
  • Different ways to ask: You can type, speak, or share a relevant prescription or health image.
  • Medicine made simpler: Understand the medicines already prescribed to you, including how and when they are usually taken.
  • Gentle support for skin concerns: Receive general care information and guidance on when professional attention may be appropriate.
  • Helpful reminders: Keep track of medicines with reminders at the times you choose.

⚠️ Safety Guardrails

  • Emergency keywords (chest pain, difficulty breathing, suicidal thoughts, stroke, heart attack) trigger immediate emergency redirect (call 112 India).
  • No dosage prescribing beyond your existing prescription.
  • Always encourages professional medical advice when personal care is needed.
  • Never presents general information as a confirmed diagnosis or a new prescription.
  • Your conversations and profile are intended for your own Care Companion experience.

📜 Terms & Privacy (summary)

  • Please provide accurate information so your Care Companion experience can be as helpful as possible.
  • Please use Care Companion responsibly and only for appropriate health-related questions.
  • Information may not always be perfect, so verify important personal medical decisions with a qualified professional.
  • Your information is intended to remain private within your Care Companion experience.
  • This is not an emergency service. For emergencies, call 112 or your local emergency service.
